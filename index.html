Privacy summary

Mochi Battery does not require registration and is designed to operate without collecting, transmitting, selling, or sharing your personal information.

No account required You can use the app without creating an account or providing your name, email address, or phone number.
No advertising or analytics SDKs The release app is not described as using third-party advertising, tracking, analytics, or profiling services.
No internet permission The Android release does not request internet, Wi-Fi state, or network state access.
Settings stay on your device Battery style, widget, and floating-badge preferences are stored locally so the app can provide its features.

1. Information we collect

Mochi Battery is designed so that its core functions can be used without sending personal data to us. We do not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, address, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

The app may read device information that is necessary to display its battery-related functionality, such as the current battery level or charging status. This information is used locally on your device to render the selected battery style or widget and is not transmitted to us.

2. Android permissions and foreground service

Mochi Battery may request Android system permissions only when required for its floating battery badge and related functionality.

Display over other apps

If you enable the floating battery feature, Android may ask you to allow Mochi Battery to display over other apps. This permission lets the selected battery badge appear above other apps. You can disable this permission at any time in Android Settings.

Foreground service

When the floating badge is active, Android may require a foreground service so the floating element can continue functioning reliably. This service is used only to support the visible floating-battery feature.

Home-screen widgets do not require the display-over-other-apps permission.

Permissions not requested by the Android release

  • Internet access
  • Accessibility service access
  • Notification-listener access
  • Contacts
  • Camera or microphone
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Wi-Fi state or network state
  • Wallpaper access

3. Local storage and preferences

Mochi Battery may save app preferences locally on your device, including your selected battery design, character, theme, widget preferences, floating-badge settings, and similar customization choices.

This information is used to remember your settings and provide the app experience you selected. The Android release disables cloud backup, so the app is not intended to upload these preferences through Android's cloud backup mechanism.

4. Third-party services, advertising, and tracking

Mochi Battery is not intended to use third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, analytics SDKs, or services that collect personal information for advertising or profiling.

If a future version adds a service that changes how data is handled, this Privacy Policy and the applicable store privacy disclosures will be updated before or when that change becomes effective.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Because the current release is designed not to collect personal information from users, we also do not share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.

6. Data retention and deletion

Since Mochi Battery does not maintain a user account or server-side user profile, there is generally no personal account data for us to retain or delete.

Locally stored app preferences can be removed by clearing the app's storage/data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

7. Children's privacy

Mochi Battery does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's cute characters and visual design do not change the fact that the app is designed to function without requiring users to submit personal information.

Parents or guardians who believe that information has been provided to us in error may contact us using the method described below.

8. Security

We use a privacy-minimizing design by keeping app preferences and battery-display functionality local to the device whenever possible. No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free, but reducing unnecessary data collection also reduces the amount of user information exposed to such risks.

9. iOS version

The iOS version may provide a different feature set because iOS does not support Android-style floating overlays above other apps. If iOS-specific features or permissions are introduced, the app and this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes, legal requirements change, or new features are added. Any revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Mochi Battery's privacy practices, please contact us using the developer support contact listed on Mochi Battery's Google Play Store or Apple App Store listing.
